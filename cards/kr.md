# Korea AI Basic Act

> Korea's binding AI framework sets notice and labelling rules, high-impact AI duties, and safety controls for a narrowly defined class of advanced systems.

**Jurisdiction:** South Korea (`KR`) · **As of:** 4 Nov 2026 · **Review:** draft-unreviewed ([reviewer seat open](../REVIEWERS.md))

**Who it hits:** AI developers and businesses providing AI products or services in Korea, including foreign operators serving Korean users, plus public bodies using high-impact AI.

**Status:** Binding; the Act and Enforcement Decree have applied since 22 Jan 2026. MSIT announced an enforcement grace period of at least one year; 22 Jan 2027 is only the earliest possible end, not a fixed date.
