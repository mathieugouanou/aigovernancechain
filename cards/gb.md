# UK AI regulation

> No general cross-sector AI Act; existing data, online-safety and criminal laws apply through sector regulators.

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (`GB`) · **As of:** 4 Nov 2026 · **Review:** draft-unreviewed ([reviewer seat open](../REVIEWERS.md))

**Who it hits:** AI developers and deployers, controllers making significant automated decisions, in-scope online services, and public bodies using algorithmic tools.

**Status:** Binding UK GDPR ADM safeguards apply from 5 February 2026. Online-safety duties cover specified services, not every standalone chatbot. England-and-Wales intimate-image offences are in force; Scotland child-image-generator provisions are enacted but not commenced. The ATRS is a mandatory government policy for specified central bodies, not a statute.
