# China AI Governance Rules

> China regulates public-facing AI through service-specific filing, safety, data, user-protection and AI-content-labelling rules.

**Jurisdiction:** China (`CN`) · **As of:** 4 Nov 2026 · **Review:** draft-unreviewed ([reviewer seat open](../REVIEWERS.md))

**Who it hits:** Providers of algorithmic recommendation, deep-synthesis, generative AI and human-like interactive services in China, plus content-distribution and app-distribution platforms; each rule has its own scope.

**Status:** Binding rules apply. GB 45438-2025 and the content-labelling measures apply from 1 Sep 2025; the amended Cybersecurity Law applies from 1 Jan 2026; human-like interactive-service rules apply from 15 Jul 2026. Comprehensive AI legislation remains a proposed work-plan item.
