# Council of Europe AI Framework Convention (CETS No. 225)

> A human-rights treaty requiring its Parties to govern AI lifecycle activities through safeguards, transparency, oversight, remedies and risk management.

**Jurisdiction:** Council of Europe (`COE`) · **As of:** 4 Nov 2026 · **Review:** draft-unreviewed ([reviewer seat open](../REVIEWERS.md))

**Who it hits:** Treaty Parties must implement the Convention for public authorities and private actors acting on their behalf; each Party chooses how to address other private actors under Article 3.

**Status:** Treaty, not yet in force as of 4 November 2026. The EU and Bosnia and Herzegovina have ratified; Article 30(3) requires five consents including three Council of Europe member States, followed by three months.
