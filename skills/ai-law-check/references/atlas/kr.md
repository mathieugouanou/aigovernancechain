# Korea AI Basic Act (KR)

As of 2026-11-04. Review: draft-unreviewed. Binding; the Act and Enforcement Decree have applied since 22 Jan 2026. MSIT announced an enforcement grace period of at least one year; 22 Jan 2027 is only the earliest possible end, not a fixed date.

## AGC-KR-AIBA-31-1: Tell users in advance when a service uses generative or high-impact AI
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 31(1); Enforcement Decree Art. 23(1), (4) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: Products or services using generative AI or high-impact AI; the Decree's exceptions apply.
- What: An AI business operator providing a product or service using generative or high-impact AI must notify users in advance that the product or service is operated using that AI. The Decree permits several notice methods and provides exceptions, including where AI use is evident, use is solely for the operator's internal business, or the Minister designates an exception.
- Controls: ai_disclosure
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/eng/engLsInfoR.do?lsiSeq=268543

## AGC-KR-AIBA-31-2: Identify outputs generated by generative AI
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 31(2); Enforcement Decree Art. 23(2), (4) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses generated-media, deepfake; conditions: Covers generative-AI outputs; Decree exceptions may apply, including evident AI use and solely internal business use.
- What: An AI business operator providing generative AI or a product or service using it must indicate that the output was generated by generative AI. The Decree permits human-perceptible or machine-readable indications; a machine-readable indication must be accompanied at least once by text, voice, or another means telling users that the content was AI-generated.
- Controls: content_marking
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=288781

## AGC-KR-AIBA-31-3: Clearly label realistic synthetic audio, images, and video
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 31(3); Enforcement Decree Art. 23(3), (4) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses deepfake, generated-media; conditions: AI-generated virtual sound, images, or video difficult to distinguish from real content; Decree exceptions may apply.
- What: If an operator provides AI-generated virtual sound, images, or video that are difficult to distinguish from real content, it must notify or label users so they can clearly recognize that the content was generated by AI. For artistic or creative work, the label may be presented in a way that does not hinder its exhibition or enjoyment; the Decree requires recognition by visual, auditory, or software means and consideration of the primary users' age and circumstances.
- Controls: deepfake_label
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=288781

## AGC-KR-AIBA-32: Manage lifecycle safety risks for qualifying advanced AI systems
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 32(1)-(3); Enforcement Decree Art. 24 · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses frontier-model; conditions: All three Decree Art. 24 criteria must be met; the compute threshold alone does not trigger the duties.
- What: For an AI system meeting the Decree's cumulative training-compute threshold, the operator must identify, assess, and mitigate risks throughout the lifecycle, maintain a risk-management system to monitor and respond to AI safety incidents, and submit implementation results to MSIT. The Decree requires all criteria together: at least 10^26 floating-point operations for training, use of the most advanced AI technology then in use, and a likelihood of widespread and significant effects on life, physical safety, or fundamental rights.
- Controls: risk_management, incident_reporting
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=288781

## AGC-KR-AIBA-33: Review whether a system is high-impact AI before providing it
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 33(1)-(4); Enforcement Decree Art. 25 · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: Applies where AI is provided, including systems used in statutory areas that may significantly affect or pose risk to life, physical safety, or fundamental rights.
- What: When providing AI or a product or service using it, an AI business operator must review in advance whether the system qualifies as high-impact AI. If classification is uncertain, the operator may request confirmation from the Minister of Science and ICT; the Decree specifies the request materials and a response period of 30 days, extendable once by up to 30 days.
- Controls: risk_management, technical_documentation
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/eng/engLsInfoR.do?lsiSeq=268543

## AGC-KR-AIBA-34: Apply safety and trust measures to high-impact AI
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 34(1)-(3) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: Applies to high-impact AI or products or services using it; classification depends on statutory domains and risk criteria.
- What: An operator providing high-impact AI, or a product or service using it, must implement measures under the Decree that include a risk-management plan; technically feasible explanations of final outputs, main criteria, and an overview of training data; user-protection measures; human management and oversight; and records verifying the measures. Equivalent measures under other laws may be deemed to satisfy the duty as prescribed by the Decree.
- Controls: risk_management, transparency_notice, human_oversight, technical_documentation
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/eng/engLsInfoR.do?lsiSeq=268543

## AGC-KR-AIBAD-27-1-2: Publish key high-impact AI safeguards and retain evidence for five years
- Rule: AI Basic Act Enforcement Decree Enforcement Decree Art. 27(1)-(2); Act Art. 34(1) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: Applies to operators of high-impact AI or products or services using it.
- What: An operator must post the main contents of its risk-management, explanation, and user-protection measures, plus the name and contact details of the human supervisor, at its office or on its website. It must retain supporting documents for the Article 34 measures for five years; trade secrets may be omitted from the public posting, but the retention duty remains.
- Controls: transparency_notice, human_oversight, technical_documentation, audit_log
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=288781

## AGC-KR-AIBA-35-1: Endeavor to assess fundamental-rights impacts of high-impact AI
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 35(1); Enforcement Decree Art. 28 · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: The Act frames the operator's assessment duty as an effort duty; public bodies separately must prioritize products or services that have undergone an assessment.
- What: An operator providing products or services using high-impact AI must endeavor to assess in advance the impact on people's fundamental rights. The Decree lists assessment elements including affected people and rights, social and economic effects, use patterns, evaluation metrics, risk prevention or mitigation, loss recovery, and improvement plans.
- Controls: impact_assessment
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1031810855

## AGC-KR-AIBA-35-1-VG: Reflect AI-vulnerable groups in impact assessments
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 35(1) second sentence; Enforcement Decree Art. 28(1)(1) · status applicable · applies from 2026-07-21 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: Applies when an operator performs the Article 35 impact assessment; the assessment must account for vulnerable-group characteristics in light of the product or service.
- What: When an operator carries out an impact assessment for high-impact AI, the assessment must reflect the characteristics of AI-vulnerable groups, taking account of the nature of the product or service. The amended sentence took effect on 21 July 2026.
- Controls: impact_assessment
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1031810855

## AGC-KR-AIBA-35-2: Prefer high-impact AI products or services with impact assessments
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 35(2) · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use; conditions: Applies to covered State agencies, local governments, and public institutions procuring or using high-impact AI products or services.
- What: When State agencies and other covered public institutions intend to use products or services using high-impact AI, they must give priority consideration to products or services that have undergone an impact assessment.
- Controls: impact_assessment

## AGC-KR-AIBA-36-1: Appoint a domestic agent when foreign-operator thresholds are met
- Rule: AI Basic Act Art. 36(1)-(3); Enforcement Decree Art. 29 · status applicable · applies from 2026-01-22 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: No Korean address or business office and at least one Article 29 threshold; the fourth trigger is a prior administrative fine under Act Article 43(1)(3).
- What: An AI business operator without an address or place of business in Korea must designate a domestic agent in writing and report the designation to MSIT if it meets any Decree threshold: prior-year total revenue of at least KRW 1 trillion; prior-year AI-service revenue of at least KRW 10 billion; at least one million average daily domestic users over the preceding three months as of the previous year-end; or an administrative fine for failing to comply with a corrective order. The agent handles specified safety-result submissions, high-impact classification requests, and support for Article 34 measures; the operator is treated as responsible for the agent's related violations.
- Controls: local_representative, registration, technical_documentation
- Source: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=288781
