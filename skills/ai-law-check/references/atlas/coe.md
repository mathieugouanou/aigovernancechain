# Council of Europe AI Framework Convention (CETS No. 225) (COE)

As of 2026-11-04. Review: draft-unreviewed. Treaty, not yet in force as of 4 November 2026. The EU and Bosnia and Herzegovina have ratified; Article 30(3) requires five consents including three Council of Europe member States, followed by three months.

## AGC-COE-CETS225-3: Apply the Convention to public-sector AI and address risks from other private actors
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 3(1)(a)–(b) · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, any-ai; conditions: Applies to lifecycle activities with potential to interfere with human rights, democracy or the rule of law. This is a duty of a State Party, not a directly applicable treaty duty on companies; national-defence matters and pre-deployment R&D are excluded, except testing with such potential. Other private-actor coverage follows the Party's Article 3 implementation choice.
- What: Each Party must apply the Convention to AI lifecycle activities by its public authorities and private actors acting on their behalf. For other private actors, each Party must address risks and impacts consistently with the Convention's object and purpose, and declare whether it will apply the treaty principles and obligations or take other appropriate measures.
- Controls: risk_management, privacy_compliance
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-7-9-13: Protect dignity, equality, privacy, accountability and reliability in AI activities
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Arts. 7, 9–13 · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, any-ai; conditions: State-Party measures apply to covered public-authority activities and private actors acting on their behalf; wider private-sector implementation is determined under Art. 3(1)(b).
- What: Parties must ensure AI lifecycle activities respect human dignity and individual autonomy, accountability and responsibility, equality and non-discrimination, privacy and personal-data protection, reliability, and safe innovation. The Convention requires Parties to give effect to these principles through measures consistent with the instrument and their legal systems.
- Controls: human_oversight, bias_testing, privacy_compliance, risk_management
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-8: Provide context- and risk-appropriate transparency and oversight
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 8 · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, any-ai, generated-media, chatbot; conditions: Treaty obligations are on Parties; operational requirements for providers and deployers depend on applicable domestic implementation and the Article 3 scope.
- What: Each Party must adopt or maintain measures ensuring adequate transparency and oversight requirements tailored to the context and risks of AI lifecycle activities. The Article expressly includes measures concerning identification of content generated by AI systems.
- Controls: transparency_notice, human_oversight, content_marking
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-14: Ensure effective remedies and information to challenge consequential AI use
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 14(1)–(2) · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, significant-decision; conditions: The Convention sets duties on Parties; the remedies obligation is expressly qualified by existing international obligations and the domestic legal system.
- What: To the extent required by a Party's international obligations and consistent with its domestic legal system, it must ensure accessible and effective remedies for human-rights violations arising from AI lifecycle activities. Measures include documenting relevant information, making it available to authorised bodies and, where appropriate, affected people, enabling them to contest decisions substantially informed by AI, and providing a way to complain to competent authorities.
- Controls: audit_log, transparency_notice, contest_and_appeal
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-15: Provide procedural safeguards and, where appropriate, AI-interaction notice
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 15(1)–(2) · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, significant-decision, chatbot, agent; conditions: The interaction notice is context-dependent; the treaty's procedural-safeguard requirements apply where human rights and fundamental freedoms are significantly affected.
- What: Each Party must ensure effective procedural guarantees, safeguards and rights where an AI system significantly affects the enjoyment of human rights and fundamental freedoms, consistent with applicable international and domestic law. Parties must also seek context-appropriate notice that a person is interacting with an AI system rather than a human being.
- Controls: human_oversight, ai_disclosure, contest_and_appeal
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-16: Manage AI risks and impacts iteratively across the lifecycle
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 16(1)–(4) · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, any-ai; conditions: Treaty duties are on Parties and apply to covered lifecycle activities; each Party's measures must reflect context, intended use and risk.
- What: Each Party must adopt or maintain graduated, context-sensitive measures to identify, assess, prevent and mitigate AI risks to human rights, democracy and the rule of law. Measures apply iteratively, consider impact severity and probability and relevant stakeholders, monitor and document risks and impacts, and require testing where appropriate; Parties must assess whether incompatible uses warrant a moratorium, ban or other measures.
- Controls: risk_management, impact_assessment, audit_log, security_testing
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c

## AGC-COE-CETS225-26: Establish independent and effective oversight mechanisms
- Rule: Council of Europe AI Framework Convention Art. 26(1)–(2) · status not-yet-applicable · applies from n/a · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use, any-ai; conditions: This is an institutional duty of each Party; it does not itself create an AI-authorisation or registration requirement for companies.
- What: Each Party must establish or designate one or more effective mechanisms to oversee compliance with the Convention. Those mechanisms must be independent and impartial and have the powers, expertise and resources needed to perform their duties effectively.
- Controls: risk_management, audit_log
- Source: https://rm.coe.int/1680afae3c
