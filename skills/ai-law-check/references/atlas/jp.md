# Japan AI Promotion Act and business guidance (JP)

As of 2026-11-04. Review: draft-unreviewed. The Act is fully in force; Article 7 requires cooperation with government measures but sets no penalties. The Second AI Basic Plan was adopted on 14 July 2026; METI/MIC Guidelines Ver1.2 are voluntary.

## AGC-JP-AIPA-7: Cooperate with national and local AI measures
- Rule: AI Promotion Act Art. 7 · status applicable · applies from 2025-06-04 · confidence high
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer; uses any-ai; conditions: People developing or providing AI-enabled products or services, or using AI in business activities.
- What: Utilization business operators must cooperate with measures taken by the national and local governments under Articles 4 and 5. They need only endeavor to use AI actively to improve business efficiency and advancement and create new industries.
- Controls: risk_management
- Source: https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/5066/je

## AGC-JP-AIBP-PLAN: Follow Japan's current government AI strategy
- Rule: Second AI Basic Plan Second AI Basic Plan, Chapters 1-4 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-07-14 · confidence high
- Who: roles public-body; uses public-sector-use; conditions: Government policy commitment under Article 18 of the AI Promotion Act; no direct private-sector obligation.
- What: The Cabinet's second plan sets government policy across AI adoption, development capabilities, AI governance and an AI-powered society, with implementation and follow-up arrangements. It is a government strategy, not a direct compliance duty for private AI operators.
- Controls: risk_management
- Source: https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/ai/ai_plan/aiplan_eng_20260714.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C1: Keep AI human-centric
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.1 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Nonbinding soft-law recommendations for AI business actors, including public institutions.
- What: The voluntary guidelines recommend respecting human dignity and autonomy when developing, providing or using AI, and taking steps against manipulation and automation bias. They also call for user support appropriate to the system and context.
- Controls: human_oversight, transparency_notice
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C2: Assess and manage AI safety risks
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.2 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Voluntary, risk-based guidance for AI business actors, including public institutions.
- What: The guidelines recommend checking reliability, robustness and controllability, analyzing risks, and taking proportionate measures throughout development, provision and use. They also recommend defining responses for cases where AI safety is endangered.
- Controls: risk_management, accuracy_robustness, human_oversight
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C4: Protect privacy throughout the AI lifecycle
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.4 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Voluntary guidance; applicable personal-information laws remain binding independently.
- What: The guidelines recommend observing applicable privacy laws and adopting privacy measures based on the use context and people's reasonable expectations. They also recommend a privacy policy and privacy-by-design measures appropriate to the AI system.
- Controls: privacy_compliance, data_governance
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C5: Secure AI systems and services
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.5 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Voluntary guidance for AI business actors, including public institutions.
- What: The guidelines recommend reasonable security measures suited to the technology and its lifecycle, including attention to system connections, vulnerabilities and current attack methods. The guidance does not replace binding cybersecurity or sectoral rules.
- Controls: security_testing
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C6: Provide proportionate information about AI
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.6 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Voluntary recommendations; disclosure should reflect the social context and system purpose.
- What: The guidelines recommend making AI systems verifiable and providing stakeholders with information about AI use, capabilities, limitations, risks and appropriate use, to a reasonable extent that is technically possible. They also recommend keeping relevant process and decision logs where possible.
- Controls: transparency_notice, audit_log
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf

## AGC-JP-AIGB-C7: Document AI accountability
- Rule: AI Guidelines for Business Ver1.2 Part 2.C.7 · status voluntary · applies from 2026-03-31 · confidence medium
- Who: roles provider, developer, deployer, public-body; uses any-ai; conditions: Voluntary accountability recommendations for AI business actors, including public institutions.
- What: The guidelines recommend traceability of data and decisions, assigning a responsible person, clarifying shared responsibilities, explaining conformity to the common principles and documenting relevant information. The scope of these steps should reflect the actor's role and the system's risks.
- Controls: audit_log, technical_documentation, risk_management
- Source: https://www.meti.go.jp/shingikai/mono_info_service/ai_shakai_jisso/pdf/20260331_12.pdf
