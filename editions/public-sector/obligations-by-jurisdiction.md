## Cross-border and scope cautions

- The EU AI Act's territorial scope can reach providers and deployers outside the EU in specified cases. A Swiss, UK or US body should not assume it is outside scope merely because it is not established in an EU Member State.
- The Convention on AI is a Council of Europe treaty, not a substitute for the EU AI Act, local privacy law or administrative-law safeguards.
- “High-risk” is instrument-specific. An EU Annex III system, a US OMB high-impact use case and a Canadian AIA level are not interchangeable labels.
- Government-specific guidance can bind through internal policy, funding, procurement or employment arrangements even when it is not a statute. Record the legal basis for applying it to the body.
- Verify the live law, commencement orders, local rules and current official templates before a tender or go-live. Dates and treaty status can change after this cut-off.
