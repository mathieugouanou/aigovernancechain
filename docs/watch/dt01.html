EU AI Act Article 50: AI Content Marking by 2 December 2026

EU AI Act Article 50(2) requires providers of AI systems that generate images, video, audio or text to mark outputs in a machine-readable format, detectable as AI-generated. It applies from 2 August 2026; systems placed on the market before that date have until 2 December 2026. Fines can reach EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover.

Short answers

When must AI-generated content be marked in the EU?

Since 2 August 2026 under EU AI Act Article 50(2); systems placed on the market before that date have until 2 December 2026. Draft open data; check the source. Not legal advice.

Who must mark AI-generated content?

Providers of AI systems that generate synthetic audio, image, video or text, including general-purpose AI systems.

What are the fines?

Up to EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover under Article 99(4).
