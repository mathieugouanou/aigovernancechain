AI Governance for Small Businesses: Start in 8 Hours

Governance in a Day is a one-day path from AI Governance Chain that helps a small or medium business with no governance team build a minimum viable AI governance programme in 8 hours: 4 steps of about 2 hours each, from scope to evidence. Every step uses a free command that has worked since v0.1.0 (4 November 2026) and was checked again against v0.2.0 on 9 November 2026.

The one-day plan

Block one working day for one AI system, ideally the one that acts for you most, and split it into 4 blocks of about 2 hours. You need a computer with Node.js 18 or later; step 3 also needs a GitHub repository. Each block names the command to run.

  1. Morning, 2 hours1. Find the rules that apply

    List every AI tool and agent your team uses and pick one. Then list the obligations for your role, your markets and what the system does. For a chatbot in the EU, with the roles provider and deployer, the command lists 3 obligations on 9 November 2026: the banned practices (EU AI Act Article 5) and AI literacy for staff (Article 4), both applicable since 2 February 2025, and telling people they are talking to an AI (Article 50(1)), applicable since 2 August 2026. A deployer-only run lists Articles 5 and 4.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain scope -j EU -r provider,deployer -u chatbot
  2. Late morning, 2 hours2. Write the governance file

    Create AI_GOVERNANCE.md at the root of your project: the system, its owner, the duties in scope and a control for each. Replace every "todo" with a real status, and add the AI models you use to the models section.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain init
  3. Afternoon, 2 hours3. Add the check

    Run the check and fix what fails: any failure names the missing control. Then add the Shield GitHub Action to your repository, so every pull request runs the same 16 checks. A chatbot is not an agent, so the 4 agent checks are skipped. The setup is one workflow file, linked under The tool.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain check
  4. Late afternoon, 2 hours4. Keep the evidence

    Create the Evidence Pack: EVIDENCE.md and evidence.json, a dated record of what you declared and what the check found, with a SHA-256 fingerprint of AI_GOVERNANCE.md. Share it when a client asks, and put the next review date in your calendar.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain evidence

It is a first programme, not a full assessment: the checks read your file, not the behaviour of your system, and the Evidence Pack is not an audit.

Short answers

Can a small business start AI governance in one day?

Yes, as a first programme. In 4 blocks of about 2 hours, a small team can list the rules that apply to one AI system, write AI_GOVERNANCE.md, check it and create an Evidence Pack. It is a start, not a full assessment. Not legal advice.

Does it work today?

Yes. Every command shipped in v0.1.0 on 4 November 2026 and was checked again against v0.2.0 on 9 November 2026.

What does aigovchain scope list for an EU chatbot?

With the roles provider and deployer, 3 obligations on 9 November 2026: EU AI Act Article 5 (prohibited practices) and Article 4 (AI literacy), both applicable since 2 February 2025, and Article 50(1) (telling people they are interacting with an AI), applicable since 2 August 2026. A deployer-only run lists Articles 5 and 4. Not legal advice.

What does it cost?

The commands are free and open source under Apache-2.0. They run on your computer with Node.js 18 or later, and the Shield GitHub Action runs in GitHub Actions.

Is the Evidence Pack an audit?

No. It records what was declared and what was checked, and when, with a SHA-256 fingerprint of AI_GOVERNANCE.md.

Does passing the checks mean we meet the EU AI Act?

No. The checks read your declared file, not the behaviour of your system, and a result states facts about the file only. Confirm the rules for your system at the source, with a lawyer where needed.
