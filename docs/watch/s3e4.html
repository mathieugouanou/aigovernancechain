AI Governance for Small Businesses: Start in 8 Hours

Governance in a Day is a one-day path from AI Governance Chain that helps a small or medium business build a minimum viable AI governance programme in 8 hours: find the rules, write the file, add the check, keep the evidence. Every step uses a free command that works today in the current release, v0.1.0.

The one-day plan

Block one working day for one AI system, ideally the one that acts for you most. You need a computer with Node.js 18 or later; step 3 also needs a GitHub repository. Each block names the command to run.

  1. Morning, 2 hours1. Find the rules that apply

    List every AI tool and agent your team uses and pick one. Then list the obligations for your role, your markets and what the system does. The example below is a chatbot used in the EU; on 9 November 2026 it lists 2 obligations: AI literacy for staff (EU AI Act Article 4) and the banned practices (Article 5), both applicable since 2 February 2025.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain scope -j EU -r deployer -u chatbot
  2. Midday, 2 hours2. Write the governance file

    Draft AI_GOVERNANCE.md at the root of your project: the owner, the rules in scope, the controls, limits as numbers, who can stop the system and your red line. Add the AI models you use to the models section.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain init
  3. Afternoon, 3 hours3. Add the check

    Replace every "todo" in the file with a real status, run the check and fix what fails. Then add the Shield Action to your repository, so every pull request runs the same 16 checks. The setup is one workflow file, linked under The tool.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain check
  4. Last hour4. Keep the evidence

    Create the Evidence Pack: EVIDENCE.md and evidence.json, a dated record of what you declared and what the check found. Share it when a client asks, and put the next review date in your calendar.

    npx github:mathieugouanou/aigovernancechain evidence

It is a first programme, not a full assessment: the checks read your file, not the behaviour of your system.

0:00AI governance for a small team

Welcome! No governance team? You have one day. Enough to start. AI Governance Chain's AI governance for small businesses is a four-step route from scope to evidence, designed as a minimum viable programme in 8 hours.

0:25A chatbot question on Monday

Picture the owner of a small agency that resells an AI chatbot. On Monday morning, a client asks what the EU AI Act means for the product. The agency has no legal or risk team and the owner has other work to run. She can set aside one day, but not a long programme. She needs a first map of what applies, a place to record controls, and evidence she can show the client.

0:55What is an SME governance starter?

The SME one-day starter is a one-day path that helps small and medium businesses build a minimum viable AI governance programme in 8 hours. In plain words, it turns a broad question into four connected tasks the team can complete in sequence. This is the OBLIGATION, CONTROL, GATE and EVIDENCE part of the chain: identify duties, state controls, check the file and keep the results.

1:22The 8-hour path, step by step

Follow the path from morning to evening. Step 1: run aigovchain scope with the system, role and use to list relevant obligations. Step 2: run aigovchain init to write AI_GOVERNANCE.md. Step 3: add the Shield Action so pull requests run 16 checks. Step 4: run aigovchain evidence to create the Evidence Pack. The file connects the scope to controls, and the gate records what it checks.

1:53Who can use a one-day starter?

For small-agency owners, it gives a concrete answer to a client question.

For a product lead, it creates a first record of the AI system and its controls.

For a developer, it puts checks and evidence beside the repository.

2:09Limits of an 8-hour programme

This is a first programme, not a full assessment. The Action checks the declared file, not the model behaviour. Evidence records what was declared and checked; it is not an audit. Confirm the relevant rules and sources for your system. This is governance guidance, not legal advice in any jurisdiction.

2:26Block a day and follow the path

Block one day and follow the four steps, starting with aigovchain scope; the link is in the description.

Short answers

Can a small business start AI governance in one day?

Yes, as a first programme. In 8 hours a small team can list the rules that apply to one AI system, write AI_GOVERNANCE.md, check it and create an Evidence Pack. It is a start, not a full assessment. Not legal advice.

What does it cost?

The commands are free and open source under Apache-2.0. They run on your computer with Node.js 18 or later, and the Shield Action runs in GitHub Actions.

Does passing the checks mean we meet the EU AI Act?

No. The checks read your declared file, not the behaviour of your system, and a result states facts about the file only. Confirm the rules for your system at the source, with a lawyer where needed.
