The script

The script of this episode as of 9 November 2026, ahead of the video, updated to match the roadmap: the generator is planned, not released. Numbers are written as digits. Sources are listed below the script.

0:00AI-BOM: what sits inside?

Welcome! Which models are inside your product? Prove it. AI Governance Chain is building an AI-BOM generator that turns declared models and dependencies into a machine-readable inventory.

0:22The buyer's model inventory

Picture an enterprise buyer sending a supplier questionnaire on Friday. One question asks for every AI model and dataset in your product. The product team opens a spreadsheet. One model entry is old, and nobody knows what else changed. The buyer needs a list they can review, not a guess assembled at the last minute.

0:45What an AI-BOM records

An AI-BOM is a machine-readable inventory of the models, datasets and dependencies inside an AI system. In plain words, it is a structured parts list for the AI in a product. The generator will read declared models from AI_GOVERNANCE.md and the project dependencies. On the chain, this connects CONTROL to EVIDENCE: what the team declares becomes a record others can inspect. It extends a software bill of materials to AI.

1:13Create a CycloneDX or SPDX file

Step 1: open the models section of AI_GOVERNANCE.md and declare the system models. Step 2: include the project dependencies. Step 3: once v1 ships, planned for 30 November 2026, run aigovchain aibom to create the inventory. The generator is planned to offer 2 formats: CycloneDX and SPDX 3.0 with its AI profile. CycloneDX's machine-learning BOM support arrived in version 1.5 in June 2023, and Ecma International publishes CycloneDX as the standard ECMA-424. SPDX 3.0, released in April 2024, includes AI and Dataset profiles.

1:49Who uses the AI-BOM?

Procurement teams can send the inventory with a supplier response. Developers can compare declared models with project dependencies. Risk and legal teams can review the components recorded for a use case. Product owners can make the model list visible during a change review.

2:06Limits of a model inventory

The generator lists declared components. It cannot prove a model was actually used or that a dataset is authorised. Missing entries stay missing, and old entries can become stale. This is an inventory, not an audit, and it is governance guidance, not legal advice in any jurisdiction. Keep the models section current and check the source files. A machine-readable file can sit beside the product records used in a supplier review.

2:33Declare your models

Your one action today: declare your models in the models section of AI_GOVERNANCE.md, so the generator can read them when it ships.

You can list what's inside. Can you prove nobody edited the evidence afterwards?

Short answers

What is an AI-BOM?

A machine-readable inventory of the models, datasets and dependencies inside an AI system. It extends a software bill of materials (SBOM) to AI.

Which formats exist today?

CycloneDX has supported machine-learning BOMs since version 1.5 (June 2023); Ecma International publishes CycloneDX as ECMA-424 (1st edition June 2024, 2nd edition December 2025). SPDX 3.0 (April 2024) has AI and Dataset profiles.

Can I generate an AI-BOM with AI Governance Chain today?

Not yet. The aibom command is planned for v1, 30 November 2026, and is not in v0.1.0. Today you can declare your models in the models section of AI_GOVERNANCE.md.

Does an AI-BOM prove which models were used?

No. It lists declared components. It cannot prove that a model was actually used or that a dataset is authorised. Not legal advice.
