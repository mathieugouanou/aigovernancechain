# Discovery interviews (4 Nov to 31 Dec 2026)

We launched first and are learning in public. Goal: **40 interviews by 31 Dec 2026, including 10 with people who build AI agents.** Findings are logged, anonymised, in a pinned public issue and can reorder the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## Who we want to talk to

Developers and product teams shipping AI features; agent builders; compliance, risk, privacy and legal people; SME owners; public-sector teams; auditors and consultants. Any country, any size.

## Format

20 to 30 minutes, video or written. No sales pitch, nothing to buy. You can stay anonymous. We publish only what you approve.

## Interview guide

Ask about past behaviour, not opinions about our ideas. Do not show the product until the end.

1. Tell me about the last time an AI law or policy question came up in your work. What triggered it?
2. What did you do next? Who did you ask? What did you use?
3. How long did it take, and what did it cost (time, money, delay)?
4. What was the hardest part? What would have made it a non-event?
5. How do you track dates and changes today (for example the EU AI Act changes in 2026)?
6. How do you know what your AI systems and agents are allowed to do, and who can stop them?
7. Has anyone asked you to prove your AI governance (customer, auditor, board, regulator)? What did you give them?
8. *(Agent builders)* Walk me through how a tool call is approved or limited in your agent today. What happened the last time something went wrong?
9. *(End, optional)* Here is `aigovchain scope` / `check` / `jail`. Try it on your system. What is confusing? What is missing? Would you use it next week?

## Hypotheses we are testing

From our problem brainstorm (30 problems), the ones we believe are frequent, painful and valuable:

| # | Problem | Our current answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I don't know which AI laws apply to me" | `scope`, regime cards |
| 2 | Deadlines keep moving | Deadline calendar, Law Diff (v1) |
| 3 | Shadow AI: unknown AI use by staff and agents | AI inventory (v1/v2) |
| 4 | Agents act without visibility or approval | Agent rails, policy packs (v1) |
| 6 | Many frameworks, duplicated work | Crosswalks |
| 8 | Can't prove governance to customers | AI_GOVERNANCE.md, Evidence Pack |
| 10 | Labelling AI content across countries | Atlas obligations on marking and labels |
| 11 | "Is my use case high-risk?" | Classification decision trees (v2) |
| 26 | Third-party agents and MCP servers are unknown risks | Agent rails, OWASP Agentic crosswalk |

A hypothesis counts as **validated** when at least 8 of 40 interviewees describe the problem unprompted, with a recent real example.
