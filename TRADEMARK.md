## You may, without asking

- Refer to the project by name to discuss, review, teach, cite or compare it.
- Say that your file follows the AI_GOVERNANCE.md specification, or that you use the AI Governance Chain Atlas, CLI, Action, MCP server, skills or rails.
- Display badges generated by the project's tools, linked to your `AI_GOVERNANCE.md`.
- Use, implement or extend the `AI_GOVERNANCE.md` file name and specification in any product, open or commercial.

## Please do not

- Use the name, or a confusingly similar name, for your own product, service, fork or certification scheme.
- Say or imply that the project, its maintainers or a future foundation certify, endorse or approve you or your system.
- Use badge wording other than checked facts (for example "certified", "compliant" or "approved").

Forks are welcome under the licences; please give a substantially modified fork its own name.
